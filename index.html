Phone Usage Study

A 4-week personal experiment visualizing my phone behavior through interactive data.

How It Works

  • Each flash = one phone pickup
  • App frequency = proportional to usage time
  • Order = least to most used apps

Methodology

Tracked every instance of unlocking my phone and viewing the home screen over 28 days. Apps are ordered by usage duration, with flash frequency matching actual behavior patterns.
